If you share our vision of feeding a hungry world, we would love to have your help in any way that you are able.

Financial Donations

If you feel called to support this mission financially, your gift makes a direct and lasting impact. Donations help us maintain our dehydrator, cover essential operating costs, and ship life-giving food to people in need around the world.

Every contribution helps move this work forward and ensures more meals reach those who need them most. We are deeply grateful for a community that shares this vision and partners with us to make it possible.

Donate via Qgiv Venmo @SMGLEANERS

Produce Donations

We cannot carry out this mission without the support of our local growers. If you own a farm or grow vegetables at home and have excess produce, your donation can directly become a meal for a family in need around the world.

Your partnership turns surplus into nourishment and waste into hope. Contact us today to learn how you can join the team and make a lasting impact through your harvest.

Volunteer Your Time

We need your help turning donated produce into life-giving meals. Volunteers assist with washing, chopping, and preparing produce for dehydration, as well as packing and preparing finished food for international distribution. Every step of the process matters—and it truly takes a team to make this mission possible.

We also invite you to stand with us in prayer for this work. Our heart is to be used as instruments for the Lord—serving others, sharing His love, and meeting practical needs in a hurting world. We believe prayer is powerful, and we are grateful for those who join us in asking for God’s provision, strength, and blessing over this mission and those we serve.

There are volunteer opportunities available for both individuals and groups.

Contact us to learn more and find a time that works for you.
