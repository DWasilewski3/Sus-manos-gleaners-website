About Us

Our Story & Biblical Origin

What started in two vans

In 2004, a small youth group from Grand Rapids set out on a journey that would reshape their lives. Packed into two vans, high school students and their leaders drove across the continent to British Columbia’s Okanagan Valley with a simple mission: serve.

Camping in an apple orchard, they spent their days chopping surplus fruits and vegetables that would have otherwise gone to waste, spreading them onto trays for industrial dehydration. Once dried, the produce became nutritionally balanced soup mixes, ready for mission partners to feed hungry families around the world.

What began as a service trip quickly revealed something extraordinary:

  • High-yield service: Just one hour of volunteer labor yielded up to 60 servings of food.
  • Low-cost impact: Each serving cost only 10 cents to produce.
  • Zero waste: For every pound of rescued produce, three nutritious meals were created.

Inspired by their time with Okanagan Gleaners, the group returned home with a vision. In 2008, Sus Manos Gleaners was officially founded to mobilize West Michigan’s rich agricultural resources and strong volunteer spirit to feed a hungry world.

Today, Sus Manos Gleaners partners with local farmers, businesses, and dedicated volunteers to turn surplus produce into nutrient-dense soup mixes and dried fruit snacks. By dehydrating the food, we lock in vital nutrients, extend shelf life for years, and drastically reduce shipping weight and costs. Packaged in bulk barrels, these life-giving meals are distributed worldwide through trusted Christian mission partners to communities facing critical shortages.

What started in two vans has grown into an enduring global movement. As new doors continue to open, we invite you to join us, lend a hand, feed the hungry, and be part of something lasting.

We serve in Christ’s name, as His hands.
